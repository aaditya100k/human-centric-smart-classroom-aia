auto_delete B. Data Retention Policies & Automated Deletion

Zero-persistence volatile frame buffers and automated retention expiration schedules.

verified Data Minimization & Deletion Guarantees:

  • Raw Camera Frames: Retained for exactly 0 seconds. Decoded into volatile OpenCV numpy memory buffers and immediately released upon feature vector extraction.
  • Biometric Vectors: Stored as 128-dimensional floating-point vectors only. Irreversible; original photographic facial geometry cannot be reconstructed from the vector.
  • Automatic Expiration: Attendance records older than the configured threshold (default 365 days) are automatically purged during periodic scheduled runs.
